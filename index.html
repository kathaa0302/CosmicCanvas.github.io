The person behind the canvas

Hello, curious minds.

I hold a Master's degree in Astrophysics and Cosmology from the University of Padova, Italy, and a Bachelor's degree in Engineering Physics from Bennett University, India.


My scientific journey has taken me into the world of Hubble Space Telescope data, exoplanets, and planetary detection. But my curiosity doesn't end with the stars. I love painting portraits, practising yoga, singing, and experimenting with Indian and continental fusion cooking.


Cosmic Canvas is where all these different parts of me come together.
